Good to know

FAQ.

How is BMI calculated?

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². For example, 70 kg ÷ (1.75 m × 1.75 m) = 22.9.

What do the categories mean?

Under 18.5 is underweight, 18.5–24.9 is healthy, 25–29.9 is overweight, and 30 or above is obese — the standard adult WHO ranges.

Is BMI a perfect health measure?

No. BMI doesn’t separate muscle from fat, so very muscular people can score high while being healthy. Treat it as a screening guide, not a diagnosis.

Is my data saved anywhere?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and never leaves your device.
